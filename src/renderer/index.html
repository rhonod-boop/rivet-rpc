This is a beta, so things will break

Rivet sets your Discord presence over the local RPC socket. The core flow works. The edges are still rough: a stale presence, a layout that clips, a reconnect after Discord restarts. If something misbehaves, hit Update once, and if that fails, reconnect.

  1. Discord desktop has to be running. Browser and mobile clients can't receive RPC at all. If Rivet says it can't connect, restart Discord once and retry.
  2. You need your own application ID. Make a free app in the Discord developer portal and paste its ID. Its name and uploaded assets are what people see.
  3. Images are the flakiest part. Use an asset key from your app or a direct https link. Some hosts are blocked or slow to resolve.
  4. Buttons and streaming are up to Discord. Link buttons are hidden for some accounts, and Discord refuses the streaming type over RPC — Rivet sends Playing plus a Watch stream button instead.

Nothing here touches your account credentials — it only talks to the Discord client already running on this PC.
